Who We Are

Field-focused student societies are standard practice at leading international universities — they give students a structured way to collaborate on academic work outside the formal curriculum. MedicineBank was founded to bring that model to MNU, as a student-led academic collaboration society spanning the colleges of Medicine, Pharmacy, and Dentistry.

We are not a social club. Every activity we run is designed to build a concrete academic skill, publication, or resource that students take with them into their careers.

We operate through four sectors — Research, Media, Logistics, and Relations — each with a specific mandate and a track record to show for it.

Research

Peer-to-peer training, real output

We ran a 4-part peer-to-peer research training series covering research question formulation, research methods, and medical statistics (parts I & II) — consistently well-attended, and directly resulting in 7 completed student research methodologies.

We also hosted a joint research Q&A with Leicester University's medical association, drawing 450+ student attendees across multiple countries.

Media

MedBank Records and beyond

Our podcast arm, MedBank Records, has generated 147,000 all-time plays and downloads with 4,690 Spotify followers, alongside a growing YouTube presence.

We've hosted guest interviews — including a former national handball player now completing his medical degree in the US, talking about personal experience in that regard — and continue to cover MedicineBank's campaigns and public events.

Logistics

The technical backbone

This sector runs MedicineBank.org, arranges every event we host — online and in-person — and manages podcast production.

It's also the sector that incubated Patholytica, an academic content platform for medical students that went on to win recognition at Startup Grind MNU, and now stands as a live example of the kind of outcome this initiative is capable of producing when given room to build.

Relations

Every partnership, secured here

We currently have verbal agreements in place with the student union at LKC School of Medicine, Singapore (a top-ranked institution) and with the REACT initiative at Al-Kafeel University, Iraq, both aimed at joint student research publications — pending formal university endorsement.

We are also partnering with Triviadox, a student society at the Medical University of Cairo, to make MedicineBank.org more personalized for each student.
